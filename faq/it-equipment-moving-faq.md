# IT Equipment Moving FAQ

## What is IT equipment moving?

IT equipment moving involves the relocation of servers, network equipment, telecommunications systems, workstations, storage infrastructure, and technology assets between facilities.

---

## What types of technology equipment can be relocated?

Common assets include:

- Server racks
- Blade servers
- Storage arrays
- Switches
- Routers
- Firewalls
- UPS systems
- Telecommunications equipment
- Workstations

---

## What is data center relocation?

Data center relocation is the coordinated migration of technology infrastructure from one facility to another while minimizing downtime and protecting equipment.

---

## How are servers protected during transport?

Methods include:

- Anti-static materials
- Shock protection
- Rack stabilization
- Asset labeling
- Equipment verification
- Controlled loading procedures

---

## Can server racks be moved fully populated?

Project requirements determine whether equipment remains mounted or is removed before transport.
