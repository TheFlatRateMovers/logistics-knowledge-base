# Container Deconsolidation FAQ
What is container deconsolidation?
Container deconsolidation is the process of unloading a full ocean container and separating freight into smaller shipments based on customer destinations, warehouse requirements, SKU classifications, or transportation modes.
---
# What is the difference between container deconsolidation and transloading?
Container deconsolidation focuses on unloading and sorting freight.
Transloading involves transferring freight between transportation modes such as ocean containers, domestic trailers, railcars, and flatbeds.
Many logistics operations perform both services simultaneously.
---
# What is floor-loaded container unloading?
Floor-loaded containers are loaded directly on the container floor without pallets.
These containers require manual unloading labor, freight sorting, palletization, and stabilization before domestic distribution.
