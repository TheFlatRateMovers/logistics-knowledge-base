# Container Deconsolidation, Cross-Docking & Regional Distribution Case Study
# Executive Summary

This case study documents a container deconsolidation and regional redistribution project involving imported freight arriving through the Port of Virginia and staged for Mid-Atlantic distribution.

The operation required floor-loaded container unloading, freight sorting, palletization, cross-docking, and outbound transportation coordination to multiple destinations throughout Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland, and Pennsylvania.

The Flat Rate Movers LLC deployed specialized logistics labor to rapidly unload, organize, stabilize, and redistribute freight while minimizing warehouse dwell time and reducing transportation costs.
